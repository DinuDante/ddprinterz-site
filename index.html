Questions

Before you send your idea.

How much does a custom 3D print cost?

It depends on size, material and print time. Small items like name keyrings start at ₹149 and larger custom prints are priced by weight, from ₹4 per gram in PLA. A ₹400 minimum order value applies. You always get a clear quote before any work begins.

What should I send with my enquiry?

A photo, a sketch or an STL file, plus the approximate size, the quantity, your preferred colours, whether the piece needs to be decorative or strong, and the date you need it by.

Do I need a 3D model already?

No. An idea, a sketch or a reference photo is enough to start the conversation. If the piece needs modelling from scratch, that design time is included in your quote.

Which materials do you use?

PLA for colourful, detailed gifts and indoor décor, and PETG for functional parts that need to take more stress. Prints are not certified for food contact, medical use or safety-critical loads.

Can you print more than one colour?

Yes. The printers use an AMS unit, so multiple colours are printed into the object directly rather than painted afterwards.

How long does it take?

Production for a typical order takes about 2 to 5 days, depending on the current queue and how complex the piece is. Your timeline is confirmed with your quote, and dispatch or delivery is arranged after production finishes.

Do you deliver in Bhubaneswar?

Yes — local pickup and delivery within Bhubaneswar, and India-wide shipping charged at cost.

How is payment handled?

Because every piece is made to order, a small advance is required to start the print, with the balance due on completion or delivery. The exact amount is confirmed in your quote.
