FAQ

Common questions

How much does a custom 3D print cost?

Pricing depends on the size, material, and print time. Simple items like keychains start at ₹149, while custom prints are priced by weight (e.g., ₹4/g for PLA). We provide a clear quote before starting any work.

What information should I send?

Send us a photo, a rough sketch, or an STL file if you have one. Include approximate dimensions, your preferred colours, and whether the item needs to be strong (functional) or just decorative.

Do I need an STL file?

No, you don't need a 3D model. If you just have an idea, sketch, or reference photo, we can discuss the design. If it requires extensive 3D modelling, we'll include a design charge in your quote.

Which materials do you use?

We primarily print in PLA for colourful, detailed gifts and indoor décor, and PETG for stronger, more durable functional parts that can handle light heat and stress.

Can you print multiple colours?

Yes. We use Bambu Lab printers with AMS capability, allowing us to print true multicolour objects directly, without painting.

How long does printing take?

Most typical orders are completed within 2 to 5 days, depending on our current queue and the print's complexity. We will confirm the timeline with your quote.

Do you deliver in Bhubaneswar?

Yes, we offer local pickup and delivery within Bhubaneswar.

Do you ship outside Odisha?

Yes, we offer India-wide shipping at cost.

How are custom-order payments handled?

Since every piece is custom-made, we require a small advance payment to start the print, with the balance due upon completion/delivery.
