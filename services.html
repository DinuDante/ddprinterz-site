How a print is made

Four stages, start to finish.

The same four stages apply whether the piece is a keyring or a prototype housing.

1

Share a reference

A photo, a sketch, a product link or an STL file. Anything that shows what you want.

2

Design and material check

I confirm the size, whether it needs modelling work, and whether PLA or PETG suits the job.

3

Colour and print setup

Colours are chosen and loaded into the AMS, then the piece is printed in one run where possible.

4

Finishing and handover

Supports removed, edges cleaned, piece inspected, then local handover or shipping at cost.

Materials

PLA or PETG.

Two materials cover almost everything DDPrinterZ prints. I will tell you which one suits your piece when I quote it.

PLA ₹4/g

Best colour reproduction and the finest surface detail. Used for gifts, figurines, bookmarks, wall pieces and indoor décor.

PETG ₹5/g

Tougher and more heat-tolerant than PLA. Used for stands, brackets, enclosures and parts that get handled regularly.

Not suitable for note

These are FDM prints. They are not certified for food contact, medical use or safety-critical loads, and layer lines are a normal part of the finish.
